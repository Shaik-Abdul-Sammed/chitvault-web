Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, honest answers. Everything you need to know about ChitVault's security and usage.

1. Is it really free?

Yes. 100% free for individual managers. We don't charge monthly fees.

  • ✅ 1 Active Group: Up to 20 members free forever.
  • ✅ Zero Ads: No advertisements or data tracking.
2. Do I need internet?

No. It works completely offline. Your data stays on your phone.

  • 📶 Works Anywhere: Record payments in villages with zero signal.
  • 📱 On-Device: No cloud servers required to run your chit.
3. Can my members see their data?

Yes. They can log in with a member code to see their dues.

  • 👀 Passbook View: Members see their own installments and balance.
  • 📄 PDF Statement: Share complete payment passbooks over WhatsApp.
4. How do I back up?

The app does it automatically. You can also export a backup file.

  • 💾 Weekly Snapshots: Keeps 5 rolling encrypted backup copies.
  • 📤 1-Tap Export: Send backup files to Google Drive, SD Card, or WhatsApp.
5. Does it hold money?

No. It's a record book. Payments go directly to your UPI or bank.

  • 🚫 Zero Middleman: We never touch, pool, or hold your funds.
  • 🤝 Direct Settlements: Members pay cash or direct UPI to your account.
6. Is my data safe?

Yes. Your data never touches our servers because we operate zero remote databases.

  • 🔐 AES-256 Encryption: Military-grade local device encryption.
  • 📱 Hardware Security: Protected by your phone's fingerprint and PIN lock.
7. How do I install the app?

Download the APK directly from this page in 10 seconds.

  • 📥 Tap Download: Save `chitvault-latest.apk` (48 MB) to your phone.
  • ⚙️ Allow Unknown Sources: Enable installation in your Android settings if prompted.
8. Which languages are supported?

ChitVault supports 11 major Indian languages natively.

  • 🇮🇳 Supported: English, Hindi, Urdu, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam, Bengali, Gujarati, Punjabi, Marathi.
  • 🔄 Instant Switching: Toggle UI language at any time without restarting the app.
9. Can multiple managers use it?

Yes. Independent managers use ChitVault on individual phones.

  • 👔 Solo Managers: Free and Pro plans designed for individual phones.
  • 🏢 Enterprise Chit Companies: Multi-manager sync and aggregated reporting available under Enterprise.
10. What if I lose my phone?

Your records are completely safe with your weekly encrypted backup.

  • 🔄 Simple Restore: Install ChitVault on your replacement phone.
  • 🔑 Passphrase Unlock: Select "Restore Backup", enter your private key, and resume immediately.

Yes. You do not need to re-type past months from scratch.
