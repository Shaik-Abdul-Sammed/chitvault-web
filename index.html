Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, honest answers about ChitVault's architecture, security, and usage.

1. Is ChitVault really free?

Yes. The Free plan is 100% free forever for individual managers running up to 1 group with 20 members. It includes all core features — payment recording, winner calculations, PDF receipts, and 11 Indian languages. No hidden fees, no subscriptions, and absolutely no ads.

2. Does it need internet?

No. ChitVault is architected from the ground up to be offline-first. All your data, group details, calculations, and receipts are processed and stored locally on your device. You only use internet when you intentionally share a PDF receipt or send a reminder message over WhatsApp.

3. Is my data safe?

Your data never touches our servers because ChitVault operates with zero remote servers. Everything is stored on your local phone storage protected by AES-256 encryption. Your phone's biometric or PIN lock provides the first line of defense, followed by encrypted database records.

4. Can my members see their own data?

Yes. You can switch to "Member Self-Service" mode right on your phone and show members their past payment records, dues, and months remaining. You can also generate individual PDF passbooks and send them directly over WhatsApp in one tap.

5. Does ChitVault hold or manage money?

No. ChitVault is purely a record-keeping and workflow automation tool. ChitVault is not a bank, NBFC, or escrow platform. We do not hold, pool, transmit, or settle funds. All money transactions happen directly between you and your members via cash, UPI, or direct bank transfer.

6. How do I install the app?

Download the APK file directly from this website. Open the downloaded file on your Android phone. If prompted, allow "Install from Unknown Sources" or "Install Unknown Apps" in your Android settings, and complete the installation in 10 seconds.

7. Which languages are supported?

ChitVault supports 11 Indian languages: English, Hindi (हिन्दी), Urdu (اردو), Telugu (తెలుగు), Tamil (தமிழ்), Kannada (ಕನ್ನಡ), Malayalam (മലയാളം), Bengali (বাংলা), Gujarati (ગુજરાતી), Punjabi (ਪੰਜਾਬੀ), and Marathi (मराठी). You can switch languages instantly anytime.

8. Can multiple managers use it?

Individual managers use ChitVault independently on their own devices. For chit fund companies operating with multiple branch managers or field agents, our Enterprise tier offers multi-manager synchronization, centralized reporting, and role-based permissions.

9. How do I back up my data?

ChitVault features automatic weekly backups that keep 5 rolling encrypted snapshots. You can also export an encrypted backup file at any time to your Google Drive, SD card, or share it to your personal email with one tap.

10. What if I lose my phone?

As long as you have saved your weekly encrypted backup file (e.g. to Google Drive, WhatsApp, or memory card), you can install ChitVault on your new phone, select "Restore Backup", enter your secret passphrase, and resume without losing a single rupee of records.

11. Can I import my existing records?

Yes! ChitVault allows importing member rosters from your phone contact book, manual entry, or structured CSV spreadsheets. You can set the starting month of any ongoing chit so you don't have to re-enter historical payments from months ago.

12. How do I get support?

Direct support is available via WhatsApp (+91 90101 50809) and email (s9010150809@gmail.com). You talk directly to our engineering and product team based in Andhra Pradesh, India.

13. Is there a web version?

ChitVault is primarily built as an offline Android app for managers in the field. A companion web dashboard for chit fund companies and enterprise accounting teams is currently available under our Enterprise pilot program.

14. Can I use it for multiple chit groups?

Yes! The Pro and Enterprise plans allow unlimited concurrent chit groups. You can manage 5, 10, or 50 groups simultaneously with distinct installment dates, auction rules, and member lists from one unified screen.
