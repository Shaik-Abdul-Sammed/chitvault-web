Frequently asked questions

Is ChitVault free?

Yes. ChitVault is free to download and use. Core features — group management, payment tracking, winner announcements, and reports — are free forever. No ads. No subscription required.

Does it need internet?

No. ChitVault works fully offline. All data is stored on your device. You only need internet if you choose to send WhatsApp reminders or share reports.

Is my data safe?

Yes. ChitVault does not connect to any server. Your data never leaves your phone. It is protected by your device PIN, biometric lock, and encrypted local backups.

Can members see their own details?

Yes. The manager can open "View as Member" mode and hand the phone to a member to see their payments, months left, and winner history. Full member login from their own phone is coming in a future update.

Does ChitVault hold or manage money?

No. ChitVault is a record-keeping and communication tool only. It does not hold, pool, escrow, or settle funds. All payments are made directly between members and the committee manager.

How do I try it?

Message us on WhatsApp for a free demo. We can also set up a pilot for your chit group at no cost.
